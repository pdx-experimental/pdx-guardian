Honest Disclosures & Scope Boundaries

  • Lower-Bound Evidence: Experiments ran under QEMU TCG / nested KVM and 1-OCPU cloud VMs (952 MiB RAM). Safety invariants (zero collisions, zero unsafe commands, 100% recovery coverage) are proven under severe resource constraints; native automotive ECU upper bounds require physical target hardware.
  • SUMO 600-Vehicle Teleport Warnings: Both Guardian 600v and Pure SUMO 1.28.0 saturate the single-lane 3-junction geometry at max_concurrent_active ≈ 231–234, producing 4–12 SUMO default 300 s queue-teleport warnings.
